# Controle de aluguéis — Product context

## Product surface
A private, family-operated rental-management application. It is a product surface, not a marketing site.

## Audience
Mercês and close family members who need to understand the next operational action without technical training.

## Jobs to be done
- See the rental portfolio and attention points.
- Register, find and update a property safely.
- Check payment, tenant and contract information without cognitive overload.

## Voice
Calm, familiar, direct and reassuring. Portuguese (Brazil). Explain operational states in plain language.

## Visual intent
A warm, home-oriented workspace: composed, trustworthy and readable. The UI should feel like an organized family binder rather than a developer dashboard.

## Avoid
- Neon/terminal aesthetics.
- Cold SaaS-dashboard blue/cyan overload.
- Decorative gradients competing with operations.
- Color as the only carrier of status.
